# HRM UAT and training acceptance pack

## HR UAT

Named HR testers must create and update a worker, validate effective-dated organisation changes, archive/reactivate safely, process leave and attendance exceptions, complete recruitment-to-worker conversion, handle a restricted relations case, and export management reports. Verify another tenant and another worker's self-service data cannot be accessed.

## Payroll and finance UAT

Named payroll/finance testers must prepare inputs, lock and calculate, resolve or waive exceptions with reasons, prove preparer/approver/releaser segregation, approve and release, generate and approve a payment file, reconcile totals, export statutory liabilities, reverse a controlled rehearsal run, and inspect the full audit history.

## Employee UAT

Employees verify own-only profile, documents, leave, HR requests, notifications and payslips. Test direct URLs for another worker and confirm denial without data leakage.

Each case records tester, role, tenant, date, expected result, actual result, evidence reference, defect link and final pass/fail. Open severity-one or severity-two defects block acceptance.

## Training

HR training covers master-data validation, effective dating, leave/time operations, recruitment, confidential cases, reports and recovery paths. Payroll training covers configuration ownership, the full payroll lifecycle, exceptions, segregation, payment/reconciliation, statutory output, reversals and incident escalation. Technical-owner training covers identity admission, migrations, backups, monitoring, evidence, rollback and log redaction.

Attendance alone is not acceptance. The trainer records attendees and environment, and each operational owner completes a hands-on scenario before the corresponding training evidence is marked passed.
