# New World Cargo HRM — Milestone 1 Rerun Report

**System:** New World Cargo HRM  
**Environment:** [erp.newworldcargo.com](https://erp.newworldcargo.com)  
**Assessment date:** 23 August 2026  
**Prepared by:** **Manus AI**  
**Scope:** Attendance-derived overtime lifecycle, payroll allocation, live Timesheets projections, Overtime review, categorized navigation, and Quick Access.

## Executive result

> **Milestone 1 feature result: VALIDATED against the deployed live PostgreSQL/API implementation.**  
> **Overall HRM production-payroll decision: NOT APPROVED.**

The rerun confirmed that the previously completed September synthetic UAT state is persisted and correctly represented by the production deployment. The approved 3-hour attendance overtime row is paid and linked to the September payroll run and line. The rejected 2-hour row remains excluded and unlinked. The payroll line contains one dedicated, explainable overtime earning of **ZMW 64.90**. A replay-safe attempt to change the already-paid row was rejected with HTTP 422 and did not mutate the record.

A new payroll run was not created during this rerun. The live pay-group period list contains only August and September 2026; August is closed/reconciled and September already has the released synthetic UAT run. Creating another run in those periods would not be an isolated test. Therefore, the fresh write portion of this rerun was intentionally limited to the expected paid-record immutability rejection. The full pending → approved/rejected → calculated → approved → released lifecycle had already been executed in the isolated September UAT run and was revalidated here through persisted state, payroll lines, audit history, and UI projections.

## 4. Replay-safe lifecycle guard

A controlled authenticated attempt was made to reject the already-paid 3-hour record. The live API returned:

```text
HTTP 422
code: overtime-already-paid
message: This overtime record is already linked to a payroll run and cannot be changed.
```

A subsequent attendance read confirmed that the paid row remained paid and linked, while the rejected row remained rejected and unlinked. This validates the paid-record immutability guard without altering historical UAT payroll data.

## 5. Live browser validation

The deployed [Timesheet summary page](https://erp.newworldcargo.com/hrm/time/timesheets) was tested with the custom range **1–30 September 2026**. It displayed the **Live PostgreSQL attendance** indicator, two live records, two present days, and two overtime entries. The Table view showed the 3-hour paid row and 2-hour rejected row with their shift and lifecycle statuses.

The same live records were projected successfully into the Week and Month layouts. The Week layout placed the records on 3 and 4 September and displayed the clock interval, worked time, and overtime in each day cell. The Month layout displayed `OT` on days 3 and 4 and showed **5h overtime** and **21h total** for the employee. Clicking cells in both projections opened the shared attendance detail drawer rather than a separate or synthetic data path.

The paid detail drawer showed 11 hours worked, 3 hours overtime, source `device-import`, `Paid in payroll`, and the exact payroll run and line IDs. The rejected drawer showed 10 hours worked, 2 hours overtime, source `device-import`, `Excluded from payroll`, and the rejection note. No mock or preview content was rendered. The live browser console had no runtime errors during these checks.

## 6. Overtime review UI

Following the real Overtime review shortcut opened [Overtime review](https://erp.newworldcargo.com/hrm/time/operations) without a crash. The page rendered the workflow **Import → Review → Approve → Payroll**, automatic organisation/branch scope messaging, and the lifecycle tabs **Needs review, Approved, Rejected, Paid, and All records**.

The persisted dataset showed zero records needing a decision, one approved record with 9.50 eligible hours, one rejected record, and one paid historical record with 3.00 hours. The Rejected tab showed UAT Eunice’s 2.00 hours at 1.50x as excluded from payroll with the correct rejection note. The approved and paid historical records remained available for review.

Because no safe pending record existed in an isolated open period, the rerun did not fabricate a pending row or mutate a released period merely to exercise a UI approval click. The earlier isolated September UAT execution remains the evidence for the actual decision writes and separate payroll-role approval/release.

## 7. Navigation and Quick Access

The categorized sidebar was visible throughout the browser validation with the New World Cargo HRM groups **People, Time & attendance, Recruitment, Payroll & benefits, Performance, Approvals, Reports & exports, and Configuration**.

Quick Access opened from the top navigation and displayed real role-aware route links. Searching for `overtime` reduced the dialog to the real **Overtime review** shortcut. Escape closed the dialog. Following the shortcut navigated to the live Overtime review route. No dead link or runtime crash was observed in this sequence.

## 8. Limits and readiness decision

This rerun validates the **Milestone 1 feature path**, not the full HRM payroll-readiness checklist. The formal assessment remains **NOT APPROVED for production payroll**. The live readiness decision is still blocked by operational and governance evidence, including monitoring and alert validation, incident-runbook walkthrough, rollback rehearsal, HR and payroll business acceptance, administrator and operator training, and accountable-owner sign-offs. Broader stakeholder requirements for sick-note registers, probation monitoring, overtime variance reporting, and attendance-exception reporting also remain unproven or incomplete.

The following conclusions are therefore intentionally separated:

| Conclusion | Status |
|---|---:|
| Attendance-derived overtime lifecycle and payroll allocation | **Validated for the synthetic UAT fixture** |
| Paid-row immutability and replay-safe protection | **Validated** |
| Live Timesheets day/week/month/custom projections | **Validated for persisted UAT records** |
| Overtime review and history presentation | **Validated for persisted UAT records** |
| Quick Access and categorized navigation | **Validated** |
| Full payroll-readiness checklist | **Not passed** |
| Production approval to process real payroll | **NOT APPROVED** |

No real employee data, bank transfer, payment file, or external payment action was used in this rerun.
